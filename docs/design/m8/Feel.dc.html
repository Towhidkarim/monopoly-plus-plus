Card feel

Hover the card: it lifts and tilts toward the cursor with a light sheen, and settles with a spring when you leave. At rest it sways a little.

Timings: tilt follows in 120 ms, springs back in 500 ms; deal 620 ms; flip 560 ms; stamp 420 ms with a 300 ms shake. With reduced motion, sway, tilt and shake are off.
